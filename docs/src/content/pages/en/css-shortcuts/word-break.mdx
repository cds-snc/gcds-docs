# Word break

The word break class sets the `word-break` property. It controls how words or unbreakable strings break when reaching the end of a line.

<ShortcutsTable shortcuts={shortcuts} />

## Examples

### Normal<br />`break-normal`

<CodePreview demoClass="container-xs b-sm b-default p-550">
  <p class="break-normal">
    These are long words: supercalifragilisticexpialidocious and
    Sutton-under-Whitestonecliffe. This text breaks only at natural breakpoints,
    like spaces and hyphens. If there are no natural breakpoints, long words may
    overflow the container.
  </p>
</CodePreview>

### Word<br />`break-word`

<CodePreview demoClass="container-xs b-sm b-default p-550">
  <p class="break-word">
    These are long words: supercalifragilisticexpialidocious and
    Sutton-under-Whitestonecliffe. This text breaks long words when necessary to
    prevent overflow, by breaking anywhere within the word if no natural
    breakpoints (spaces and hyphens) exist.
  </p>
</CodePreview>

### All<br />`break-all`

<CodePreview demoClass="container-xs b-sm b-default p-550">
  <p class="break-all">
    These are long words: supercalifragilisticexpialidocious and
    Sutton-under-Whitestonecliffe. This text breaks at any point, even in the
    middle of a word.
  </p>
</CodePreview>
