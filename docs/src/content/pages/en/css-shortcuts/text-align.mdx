# Text align

The text align class sets the `text-align` property. It controls the horizontal alignment of text within its container.

<ShortcutsTable shortcuts={shortcuts} />

## Examples

### Centre<br />`text-center`

<CodePreview demoClass="container-full b-sm b-default p-150">
  <p class="text-center"> This text is centred within its container. </p>
</CodePreview>

### Justify<br />`text-justify`

<CodePreview demoClass="container-xs b-sm b-default p-150">
  <p class="text-justify">
    This text is stretched to align evenly along the right and left edges of its
    container.
  </p>
</CodePreview>

### Left<br />`text-left`

<CodePreview demoClass="container-full b-sm b-default p-150">
  <p class="text-left">
    This text is aligned to the left edge of its container.
  </p>
</CodePreview>

### Right<br />`text-right`

<CodePreview demoClass="container-full b-sm b-default p-150">
  <p class="text-right">
    This text is aligned to the right edge of its container.
  </p>
</CodePreview>
