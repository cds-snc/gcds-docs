# Background colour

The background colour class sets the `background-color` property. It controls the colour of an element’s background.

<Notice title="Use with caution" html={`<gcds-link href="/en/styles/colour#background">View GCDS colour guidance</gcds-link> to choose the right background colour for your context.`} />

<ShortcutsTable shortcuts={shortcuts} />
